The Obaatanpa Project, led by Madam Angela Ahimah, donated 100 crates of eggs to the Wassa Akropong Government Hospital on Tuesday, to support patients' nutrition and ease the hospital's food supply burden.

The donation forms part of the project's goal to promote health and wellness across the Amenfi East Municipality.

<p class="pullquote">The OBAATANPA Project is about care, compassion, and commitment to community development. We believe good health is a foundation for progress, and this small act is to show our love and appreciation for the dedicated staff and patients of this hospital.</p>

Receiving the items on behalf of the hospital, Mr. Agbesi Doke, Health Services Administrator, thanked Madam Angela and her team for their contribution, noting that support of this kind encourages healthcare workers to continue serving the community diligently.

Miss Catherine Ayiah, Head of the ENT Department, also thanked the project for the gesture, describing it as timely and impactful, and said hospital staff were grateful for the continuous support the initiative extends to the facility.
